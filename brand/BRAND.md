# Axiom Brand Guidelines

**Product:** Axiom — always-on agent. Desk to pocket.  
**Founder:** Vaibhav Sharma (vaibhavjnf / hackdomland@gmail.com)  
**Status:** Pre-Kickstarter / brand locked

---

## Voice

Axiom speaks like honest engineering: calm, precise, human. We describe what the device *is* and what it *does* — not what we wish AI marketing sounded like.

**Tone attributes**
- Quiet confidence (not hype)
- Physical clarity (materials, dimensions, battery)
- Continuity across contexts (desk → pocket → call)
- Respect for the user’s attention

**We say**
- “Always-on agent. Desk to pocket.”
- “Joins your phone calls over Bluetooth.”
- “About half a day of battery — designed to charge overnight.”
- “Coming to Kickstarter.”

**We do not say**
- Fake metrics, invented users, or “trusted by X”
- Fake ship dates or “shipping Qx 20xx” as fact
- “Revolutionary,” “disrupts,” holograms, or sci-fi theater
- Overclaiming autonomy (“replaces your team”)

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#F5F5F7` | Near-white, Apple-adjacent canvas |
| Text primary | `#1D1D1F` | Near-black |
| Accent / aluminum | `#A1A1A6` | Soft aluminum, secondary UI |
| Surface / card | `#FFFFFF` | Elevated panels on `#F5F5F7` |
| LED (product) | Warm white | Hardware only — not a brand hex |

Use accent sparingly: dividers, captions, inactive states. Never neon accents.

---

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** lowercase `axiom` — never “AXIOM” or “Axiom” in logo lockups.
- **UI / web:** SF-like system stack — `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- **Weights:** Regular / Medium for body; Semibold sparingly for section titles
- **Tracking:** Slightly tight on large display headlines; normal on body
- **No** decorative display fonts, no techy monospace for brand headlines

## Messaging pillars

### 1. Harness
Axiom is a physical harness for an always-available agent — microphone array, speaker, radio, power — so intelligence is not trapped behind a phone unlock.

### 2. Voice
Voice-first by design. Speak naturally at the desk or on the go. No fake holograms; clear mic path and honest acoustics.

### 3. Presence
A Ø78 × 28 mm matte aluminum puck that belongs on a desk and fits in a pocket. MagSafe underside for desk docking. Visible as an object, not an app icon.

### 4. Continuity
Same agent from desk to pocket. Bluetooth HFP call bridging so Axiom can join the user’s phone calls. One presence across contexts.

## Do / Don’t

**Do**
- Show real industrial design (matte aluminum, flush USB-C, MagSafe ring)
- State battery honestly (~½ day / ~12h design target from 4000 mAh / ~15 Wh)
- Use photography and exploded views that match the BOM
- Keep copy short; let materials and geometry sell

**Don’t**
- Invent testimonials, waitlist numbers, or partner logos
- Promise multi-day battery or cellular unless the BOM changes
- Use gradient AI blobs, neural-net wallpaper, or robot mascots
- Uppercase the wordmark in brand lockups
