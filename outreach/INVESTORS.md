## Thesis — who to pitch

Axiom sits at **consumer electronics × AI agents × voice hardware**.

Best-fit capital:

1. **Hardware AI / AI peripheral investors** — funds and angels who already understand BOM risk, certification timelines, and why an agent needs a physical harness in the Pro ChatGPT era.
2. **Consumer electronics angels** — operators from audio, wearables, desk accessories, MagSafe-adjacent accessories; taste for industrial design restraint.
3. **India deeptech** — India-based deeptech / hardware funds comfortable with global manufacturing (Shenzhen CM) and India+global GTM.
4. **SF / Bay Area consumer** — consumer and “new devices” angels who can open CM, audio, and crowdfunding intros.

**Honest market context for the pitch:** Agent *software* is crowded and improving fast. The bet is that a calm, Apple-level physical presence with call bridging and desk-to-pocket continuity is a durable product wedge — not that we have exclusive models.
