## The story

### The agent era is here. The hardware isn’t.

If you already use ChatGPT Pro or similar agent tools, you know the pattern: unlock phone, open app, lose context when you walk to a meeting or take a call. The model got smarter; the *place* it lives stayed a rectangle in your pocket.

Axiom is a physical harness for that agent — a quiet aluminum puck that sits on your desk (MagSafe underside) and comes with you in a pocket. Voice-first. Always-on. Designed to join your phone calls over Bluetooth HFP when you want it in the conversation.

No holograms. No fake “trusted by” logos. Honest engineering.

### What you get

- The Axiom puck (matte aluminum)
- USB-C charge cable (SKU TBD)
- iOS companion access (App Store listing timed with fulfillment)
- Firmware updates over the campaign lifecycle

### What you don’t get (yet)

- Android companion (post-v1 stretch)
- Inductive MagSafe charging (v1 charges over USB-C; MagSafe ring is magnetic dock)
- Multi-day battery claims (we design for ~12 h mixed use — about half a day)

---

## How it works

1. **Desk** — Place Axiom on a MagSafe-compatible mount or steel-friendly dock surface. Speak. Four mics listen; the speaker answers.
2. **Pocket** — Take it with you. Same agent presence.
3. **Call** — Pair with your iPhone over Bluetooth HFP. When you’re on a call, Axiom can act as the headset endpoint. The iOS app shows status and privacy controls.

## Risks & challenges (honest)

Crowdfunding hardware is hard. Read this before backing.

1. **Schedule slip** — Tooling, component lead times (Wi-Fi/BT modules, custom magnets), and certification queues often add months.
2. **Battery & RF** — Meeting ~12 h mixed use and stable HFP on iOS requires EVT characterization; we may tune power modes or antenna after measurement.
3. **Acoustics** — Four-mic array + small speaker in Ø78 mm needs iteration; voice quality is a risk we own in the open.
4. **MagSafe compatibility** — Magnet ring is MagSafe-*compatible* mechanically; not an Apple-licensed accessory; dock fit may vary by third-party mounts.
5. **Software** — iOS companion and agent linking depend on App Store review and third-party AI APIs — outages or policy changes can affect features.
6. **COGS & FX** — Aluminum, batteries, and freight move; tier pricing may need adjustment before launch if quotes worsen.
7. **Single founder bandwidth** — Early stage; hiring with raise/campaign funds is part of the plan.

We commit to transparent Kickstarter updates — including bad news.

## FAQ

**Is Axiom shipping now?**  
No. Coming to Kickstarter. This document is a draft.

**Does it charge wirelessly via MagSafe?**  
v1 charges over USB-C. The MagSafe-compatible ring is for magnetic docking.

**Android?**  
Not in v1 scope. Interest will be surveyed; no promise.

**How long does the battery last?**  
Design target ~12 hours mixed use (~½ day) from ~4000 mAh / ~15 Wh. Final number after EVT measurement.

**Can it join Zoom/Meet calls?**  
It joins via **Bluetooth HFP to your phone** — so if the call is on your iPhone (cellular or VoIP that uses the phone’s call/audio path as HFP), Axiom can be the headset. Exact VoIP app behavior varies; we’ll document tested apps as we validate.

**Is my call recorded?**  
Product policy: clear in-app status and consent. Do not assume silent recording — that would be wrong and often illegal. Final privacy model documented in the iOS app.
