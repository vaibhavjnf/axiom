Problem

Your best agent still lives behind a locked screen.

ChatGPT-class assistants are powerful — and trapped in phones and tabs. You unlock, type, context-switch, and lose the thread when you stand up from the desk or take a call. Axiom is a physical place for that agent to live: always reachable by voice, from desk to pocket.

Product

A Ø78 × 28 mm aluminum puck. Honest hardware.

Matte aluminum. MagSafe underside for the desk. Flush USB-C. Four MEMS mics, a 20 mm speaker, and Bluetooth HFP so it can join your phone calls. About half a day of battery — charge overnight.

Harness

Mics, speaker, radio, and power so the agent isn’t trapped in an app.

Voice

Voice-first at the desk or on the go. Clear array, no hologram theater.

Continuity

Desk to pocket. Same agent.

Axiom belongs on the desk and in the pocket. The iOS companion covers pairing, battery, and call status — so the puck stays an object, not another screen.

How it works

Desk. Pocket. Call.

1

Park it on the desk

MagSafe underside holds Axiom on a compatible dock or mount. Speak naturally — four MEMS mics listen; the agent answers through the 20 mm speaker.

2

Take it with you

Pocket the puck. Same voice-first agent, always-on, with about half a day of mixed use from the ~4000 mAh pack.

3

Join the call

Pair over Bluetooth HFP. When your phone is on a call, Axiom can bridge as the headset — mic and speaker on the desk or in hand. Manage privacy and status in the iOS companion.
